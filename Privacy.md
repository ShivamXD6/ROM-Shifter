# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 7, 2026

ROM Shifter is an open-source Android utility focused on local backup, restore, migration, and
system management.

## Privacy

* **Your data stays on your device.** ROM Shifter does not intentionally collect, sell, or upload
  your personal data to its own servers.
* **Local processing.** SMS, call logs, contacts, app data, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth data, media, and other
  backup data are processed locally when you use the corresponding features.
* **Temporary permissions.** Unlike many apps that retain sensitive permissions, ROM Shifter is
  designed to grant required permissions only when they are needed for an operation and **revoke
  temporary permissions as soon as the task is completed**, where technically possible.
* **No account required.** ROM Shifter does not require an account or registration.
* **Root access.** Some features require root access to perform system-level operations. Root access
  is only used for the operations you request.
* **Internet access.** Internet access may be used for features such as checking for or downloading
  updates. Your backup and personal data are not intentionally uploaded to ROM Shifter's servers.
* **You control your backups.** Backup files are stored locally in locations you choose. You are
  responsible for protecting or deleting copies you create or share.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when ROM Shifter's functionality or data handling changes.
